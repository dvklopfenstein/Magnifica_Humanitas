# CONTRIBUTING
Thank you for considering contributing to this project.

We currently need help shortening the flashcard deck names.

The ***Magnifica Humanitas*** flashcards are a set of decks layed out
in the same hierarchical format as the Table of Contents (TOC). For example:
```
* Magnifica Humanitas
  * Introduction
  * Chapter One: A Dynamic Approach Faithful to the Gospel
  * Chapter Two: Foundations and Principles of the Social Doctrine of the Church
  * Chapter Three: Technology and Dominance. The Grandeur of Humanity in Light of the Promises of AI
  * Chapter Four: Safeguarding Humanity at a Time of Transformation. Truth, Work, Freedom
  * Chapter Five: The Culture of Power and the Civilization of Love
  * Conclusion
```

But the TOC titles are *too long* for flashcard deck names and must be shortened for all languages:
```
* Magnifica Humanitas
  * INTRODUCTION
  * C1. A DYNAMIC APPROACH
  * C2. CHURCH SOCIAL DOCTRINE
  * C3. TECHNOLOGY & DOMINANCE
  * C4. SAFEGUARDING HUMANITY
  * C5. POWER VS LOVE
  * CONCLUSION
```
